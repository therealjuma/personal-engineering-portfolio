ABOUT

A background in operations, moving toward engineering.

My academic background is in Procurement and Supply Chain Management. My professional experience has involved practical operational work around procurement, logistics, inventory, assets and branch operations.

These experiences have shaped the kinds of problems I find interesting: how organisations operate, how information moves, how resources are allocated, and how systems can make processes more reliable.

I'm developing toward software engineering and AI engineering. My interests include Python, databases, mathematics, data systems, machine learning, automation and systems design.

I prefer learning by understanding a problem from its foundations, studying the underlying mechanisms, and building something that forces me to apply what I've learned.
